# Method extraction and evidence review

Read actual Methods, relevant parameter-response Results, tables, figures and supplement
references. Split material synthesis from formulation, crosslinking and biological tests.
Extract precursor/source/grade/lot, quantities with bases, solvent/buffer, addition order,
reaction/processing conditions, purification and endpoint, storage and characterization.
Mark a parameter not applicable when justified; missing is not zero and is not permission
to borrow from a different route. Preserve negative results and conditions of applicability.

Before reconciling conflicting methods, compare material identity, scale, target definition,
assay, sampling stage, geometry/transport conditions and equipment. Different procedures
need not be contradictory. Preserve alternative routes rather than average their values.

For each proposed modification connect:
source observation -> bounded inference -> unresolved assumption -> hypothesis ->
observable -> control -> falsification -> next decision.
Do not treat an author's mechanism interpretation as a direct measurement.

Use document ids, original numbered locators and short exact quotes. In scanned or
multi-column sources inspect the relevant page; check equation symbols and table cells.
Scope an excerpt collection as incomplete; source access is not evidence of having reviewed
all its content. If an earlier referenced protocol or supplement is needed, record the gap
and obtain it before claiming an executable procedure.
